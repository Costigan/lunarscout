Lunarscout — Typical Use Cases
Terrain & Raster Analysis
- Read/write single-band GeoTIFFs with explicit GeoReference metadata.
- Compute terrain slope, aspect, and hillshade from an elevation raster.
- Compare grids (same_grid/require_same_grid) and resample/align rasters between grids.
- Convert between raster pixel, projected, and lunar lon/lat coordinates.
Region & Candidate Screening
- Label, measure, and filter connected regions (size thresholds, 4/8 connectivity).
- Extract region borders and apply morphological opening for landing-site candidate masks.
- Screen sites by combining slope + illumination thresholds and rejecting small/disconnected regions.
Temporal Data
- Build in-memory (time, y, x) temporal cubes with UTC coordinates.
- Store/stream file-backed timestamped GeoTIFF series (writer/reader) without full-cube materialization.
- Compute streaming temporal reductions (mean, min, max, std) over cubes or file-backed series.
Celestial Geometry (SPICE)
- Compute Sun/Earth local-frame (NED) vector histories and azimuth/elevation angles.
- Obtain body elevation over terrain horizon for a point/time series.
- Plot body elevation, body paths, limbs, and zoomed paths against horizons.
Horizons & Lighting Products
- Generate CUDA-accelerated terrain horizon tiles from primary + surrounding DEMs.
- Read stored .bin/.cbin horizon tiles for individual pixels/patches.
- Generate time-series lightmaps (visible solar fraction), permanent-shadow (PSR) maps, Sun/Earth terrain-relative elevation, and safe-haven maps.
- Generate landed mission-duration products (sunlight, Sun-elevation, and Sun/Earth combinations).
Mission / Mobility Planning
- Static path planning: Dijkstra travel-time fields, A* paths with slope/slip models.
- Dynamic planning under interval occupancy (earliest-arrival paths).
- Battery-aware (state-of-charge) path planning with solar/battery/rover power models.
Map Algebra
- Eager Raster operations with explicit validity masks, units, dtype, and numeric policies (checked overflow, casts, non-finite handling).
- Lazy RasterExpression graphs with explain/plan/compute/bounded write.
- Temporal map algebra over file-backed series.
- Weighted suitability scoring, focal operations, distance, and morphological cleanup.
Scenario/Workflow Organization
- Scenario facade for filesystem-safe paths and product conveniences (dem.tif, horizons/, outputs).
- Resumable, cancellable, overwrite-safe long-running product jobs with progress callbacks.
- Map-product catalog loading/search and scenario-name sanitization for downloads.
